# LumaRing v0.1.5

- Set a separate shortcut in Settings → General → Activation to open the current app's action ring directly. Double-tap left Option still switches modes while the ring is open.
- Record single keys, function keys or key combinations. Ring shortcuts pause during recording, and conflicting shortcuts are identified in settings.
- Adjust the ring's center title size in Settings → General → Appearance, from 10–16 pt (default: 12 pt).
- Larger action names and shortcut labels, with stronger text contrast. Remove the mode-switch hint from both ring centers.

Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Open the DMG and drag LumaRing to Applications.

---

- 在设置 → 通用 → 唤起中设置独立快捷键，直接呼出当前应用的操作轮盘；轮盘打开时仍可双击左 Option 切换模式。
- 快捷键支持单键、功能键和组合键。录入期间暂停轮盘快捷键，并在设置中提示快捷键冲突。
- 在设置 → 通用 → 外观中调整轮盘中央标题字号，范围 10–16 pt，默认 12 pt。
- 放大操作名称和快捷键文字，提高文字对比度；移除两种轮盘中央的模式切换提示语。

需要 macOS 14 或更高版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel。打开 DMG，将 LumaRing 拖到 Applications 即可安装。
