# LumaRing v0.1.3

- Right-click an app to create a window, choose whether the secondary ring shows windows or tabs, or quit the app. Available actions depend on the app.
- Hold Option or double-click the center while the secondary ring is closed to show your favorite apps. Configure them in Settings → App Management.
- Choose a three- or four-finger tap to open the ring. Enable three-finger pinch in Settings to minimize the foreground window.
- Keep the ring open after closing a secondary window or tab, so you can close several in succession. Improve window preview matching, gesture recognition and browser action responsiveness.
- Sort secondary windows and tabs by their displayed names, with stable ordering for matching names.
- Right-click a secondary window or tab to close it or edit its name and sector color in a floating panel. Choose from twelve preset colors, or restore the defaults. Fix the ring disappearing and losing the target while editing.

Custom names are stored locally and tied to the current target-app process; restarting the target app starts a new naming scope. New Window requires an available command in the target application's Accessibility menu.

Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Open the DMG and drag LumaRing to Applications. The app uses the existing signing identity and is not Apple-notarized.

- 右键应用可新建窗口、选择二级轮盘显示窗口或标签页，或退出应用；可用操作取决于目标应用。
- 未展开二级轮盘时，按住 Option 或双击中心显示常用应用。在设置 → 应用管理中添加或移除应用。
- 可选择三指或四指轻点呼出轮盘，在设置中开启三指缩小，即可最小化当前窗口。
- 关闭二级轮盘的窗口或标签页后保持轮盘打开，方便连续关闭。改进窗口预览匹配、手势识别和浏览器操作响应。
- 二级轮盘中的窗口和标签页按显示名称排序，同名项目保持稳定顺序。
- 右键二级轮盘中的窗口或标签页，可关闭或打开“修改”悬浮窗，设置名称和扇形颜色，提供两排共 12 个预设颜色及恢复默认选项。修复编辑期间轮盘消失、保存失败的问题。

自定义名称保存在本机，并绑定当前目标应用进程；目标应用重启后需重新命名。新建窗口需要目标应用通过辅助功能菜单提供可用命令。

需要 macOS 14 或更高版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel。打开 DMG，将 LumaRing 拖到 Applications 即可安装。应用沿用原有签名身份，尚未经过 Apple 公证。
