# LumaRing v0.1.8

- Press Escape to dismiss the ring without switching when the shortcut is released.
- Hold-to-select now extends app selection beyond the ring in each app's direction, including outside the panel. Release the shortcut to switch.
- Expanded window and tab arcs keep selection within their side edges, even beyond the ring. Cross a side edge to choose another app.
- Keep the selected app highlighted with a release-to-switch hint. Move back to the center to cancel; no additional setting is required.
- Simplify Settings with sidebar navigation, clearer selection modes and concise, contextual help.

Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Open the DMG and drag LumaRing to Applications.

- 支持按 ESC 关闭轮盘，取消后松开快捷键不会触发切换。
- 选择“松手切换”时，应用选区沿方向延伸到轮盘外；鼠标移出轮盘窗口后仍可选择，松开快捷键切换。
- 二级轮盘展开后，选区扩展到其左右边界；移出外缘仍能选择窗口或标签页，越过侧边界再切换到其他应用。
- 按住选择时显示选中高亮和“松开快捷键切换”提示，移回中心可取消选择，无需额外设置。
- 精简设置页：使用侧边分类，明确区分点击与松手切换，将低频说明收起并按需展示。

需要 macOS 14 或更高版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel。打开 DMG，将 LumaRing 拖到 Applications 即可安装。
