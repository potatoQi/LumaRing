# LumaRing v0.1.2

- Select apps anywhere in their highlighted ring sectors, with a smaller neutral center for shorter pointer movements.
- Close a window or browser tab in the background with the **×** button in the secondary ring, without LumaRing switching to the target app.
- Drag an app icon outside the main ring and release to quit the app. Drag it back or press Escape before releasing to cancel.
- Dismiss the ring immediately on an outward drop and deliver quit requests off the UI thread.
- Show a red outline and “Release to quit” hint during an outward drag, and prevent held-shortcut release from triggering an extra switch.

Closing and quitting use the original application's normal workflow, including its save or confirmation prompts. The ring dismisses after a close or quit request.

Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Open the DMG and drag LumaRing to Applications. The app uses the existing signing identity and is not Apple-notarized.

- 一级轮盘采用完整扇形选择区，并缩小中心无选中区域，缩短鼠标移动距离。
- 点击二级轮盘右上角的 **×**，在后台关闭对应窗口或浏览器标签页，LumaRing 不会主动切换到目标应用。
- 将应用图标拖出一级轮盘后松手，退出该应用；松手前拖回轮盘或按 Escape 可取消。
- 拖出松手后立即收起轮盘，退出请求在后台发送，避免阻塞界面。
- 向外拖动时显示红色边框及“松开退出应用”提示，并避免松开呼出快捷键时额外触发切换。

关闭和退出遵循原应用的正常流程，保存或确认提示仍由原应用处理。发出关闭或退出请求后，轮盘会收起。

需要 macOS 14 或更高版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel。打开 DMG，将 LumaRing 拖到 Applications 即可安装。应用沿用原有签名身份，尚未经过 Apple 公证。
