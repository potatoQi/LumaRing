# LumaRing v0.1.1

- Add an optional four-finger trackpad tap to open or close the ring. Enable it in **Settings → General → Activation**; it is off by default.
- Recognize taps after all four fingers lift, with movement, timing and cooldown checks to reject swipes, holds and repeated activation.
- Stop gesture listening when disabled or suspended, and reconnect after wake or device changes.

The gesture feature is experimental and uses macOS's private multitouch interface. Compatibility and gesture behavior can vary by system and trackpad. Existing keyboard and mouse activation remain available.

Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Open the DMG and drag LumaRing to Applications. The app uses the existing signing identity and is not Apple-notarized.
