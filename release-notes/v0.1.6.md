# LumaRing v0.1.6

- All rings use native Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later, with a system frosted material on older versions. Improve the ring outline and contrast against light and dark backgrounds.
- Choose Regular Liquid Glass, Clear Liquid Glass or Frosted from the menu bar menu or Settings → General → Appearance. The choice is saved; older systems support Frosted only.
- Right-clicking the ring center or empty space no longer opens settings. Context menus for apps, windows and tabs remain available.

Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Open the DMG and drag LumaRing to Applications.

---

- 所有轮盘在 macOS 26 及以上使用原生液态玻璃，旧系统使用系统磨砂材质；改善浅色、深色背景下的轮廓与对比度。
- 在菜单栏菜单或设置 → 通用 → 外观中选择常规液态玻璃、通透液态玻璃或磨砂，并记住选择；旧系统仅支持磨砂。
- 右键轮盘中心或空白处不再打开设置，保留应用、窗口和标签页的右键菜单。

需要 macOS 14 或更高版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel。打开 DMG，将 LumaRing 拖到 Applications 即可安装。
