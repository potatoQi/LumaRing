# LumaRing v0.1.9

- Refine the ring with softer shadows, subtle edges and a distinct center inset.
- Add spring opening and closing animations, with a gentle lift for highlighted apps, windows and tabs. Respect the system Reduce Motion setting.
- Keep secondary windows and tabs in a compact arc of rounded sectors.
- Fix selection tint extending into the center or beyond the outer rim, while preserving mouse selection behavior.
- Remove obsolete settings migrations, unused drawing code and redundant state; retain current saved settings and macOS 14 support.

Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple Silicon and Intel. Open the DMG and drag LumaRing to Applications.

---

- 优化轮盘质感：柔和阴影、细腻边缘和更清晰的中央区域。
- 加入自然的弹性展开与收起动效，选中的应用、窗口和标签页轻微浮起；遵循系统“减少动态效果”设置。
- 二级轮盘采用紧凑的圆角扇形，保持连贯的轮盘形态。
- 修复高亮底色伸入中央圆及超出外圆边界的问题，保留原有鼠标选择手感。
- 清理过时的设置迁移、废弃绘制逻辑和冗余状态，保留当前已保存的设置及 macOS 14 支持。

需要 macOS 14 或更高版本，支持 Apple Silicon 和 Intel。打开 DMG，将 LumaRing 拖到 Applications 即可安装。
